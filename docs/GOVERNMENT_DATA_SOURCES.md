## 2. Pilot Services Specifications

### Service 1: Income Certificate (उत्पन्नाचा दाखला)
- **Slug**: `income-certificate`
- **Department**: Revenue Department, Government of Maharashtra
- **Jurisdiction**: State of Maharashtra (Applicable in Pune District)
- **Official Delivery Window**: 15 working days (Under Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act)
- **Statutory Fee**: ₹33.60 (Online processing fee via Aaple Sarkar)
- **Verification Status**: `verified` (Last verified: 2026-11-01)
- **Sources**: Aaple Sarkar Revenue Department Citizen Charter; Pune District Collectorate Public Circular.
- **Key Mandatory Documents**:
  1. Proof of Identity (Voter ID, Driving License, or Aadhaar acknowledgement)
  2. Proof of Address (Ration Card, Electricity Bill, or Telephone Bill)
  3. Income Proof (Salary Certificate Form 16, Income Tax Return, or Talathi Income Verification Report)
  4. Self-Declaration Affidavit (Form 1)

### Service 2: Domicile & Age/Nationality Certificate (अधिवास प्रमाणपत्र)
- **Slug**: `domicile-certificate`
- **Department**: Revenue Department, Government of Maharashtra
- **Jurisdiction**: Maharashtra / Pune District
- **Official Delivery Window**: 15 working days
- **Statutory Fee**: ₹33.60
- **Verification Status**: `verified` (Last verified: 2026-11-01)
- **Core Eligibility Rule**: Minimum 15 continuous years of residence in the State of Maharashtra.
- **Sources**: Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, Aaple Sarkar Portal Guidelines.

### Service 3: Caste Certificate (जात प्रमाणपत्र)
- **Slug**: `caste-certificate`
- **Department**: Revenue Department / Social Justice
- **Jurisdiction**: Maharashtra / Pune District
- **Official Delivery Window**: 21 working days
- **Statutory Fee**: ₹33.60
- **Verification Status**: `verified` (Last verified: 2026-11-01)
- **Core Eligibility Rule**: Applicant or paternal ancestors must belong to a recognized SC/ST/VJNT/OBC category with proof of residence prior to deemed cut-off year.

### Service 4: Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (नॉन-क्रीमी लेयर प्रमाणपत्र)
- **Slug**: `non-creamy-layer-certificate`
- **Department**: Social Justice and Special Assistance
- **Jurisdiction**: Maharashtra / Pune District
- **Official Delivery Window**: 21 working days
- **Statutory Fee**: ₹33.60
- **Verification Status**: `verified` (Last verified: 2026-11-01)
- **Core Eligibility Rule**: Gross annual family income across the preceding three consecutive financial years must not exceed the statutory ceiling of ₹8.00 Lakhs.

### Service 5: Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/OBC Students (MahaDBT)
- **Slug**: `mahadbt-post-matric-scholarship`
- **Department**: Social Justice & Special Assistance Department
- **Jurisdiction**: Maharashtra / Pune District
- **Official Delivery Window**: Academic Cycle
- **Statutory Fee**: Free of Cost (₹0.00)
- **Verification Status**: `verified` (Last verified: 2026-11-01)
- **Sources**: MahaDBT Official Portal Handbook (`https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in`).
